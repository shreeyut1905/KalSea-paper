\caption{\textbf{Overview of \ours.}
\textbf{(a)}~One sampler step. The \sea gate of \seacache decides whether the block stack $\blocks$ runs; the residual
$\res_i$ of a computed step updates a damped-trend Kalman filter (the predictor), whose forecast $\hat\res_i$ replaces
it on skipped steps. \textbf{(b)}~When a computed step~$b$ closes a gap, \pcgs spreads the innovation $e_b$ over the gap
with the RTS gains $g_i$. \textbf{(c)}~Re-integrating the gap with $\Emb$ and $\Out$ only reduces the deviation of the
latent from full computation at~$b$ (here $5.0\%\to2.4\%$ and $7.8\%\to3.2\%$). (b, c): one \flux generation at
$\delta=0.6$ (prompt ``McDonalds Church''); residual projected onto a random direction~$\mathbf{p}$ in the lowest DCT
band; gray: full computation.}
\label{fig:method}
