\caption{\textbf{Overview.}
\textbf{(a)}~In each step~$i$, the \sea gate of \seacache accumulates the \sea-filtered relative change $\gdist_i$ of
the first block's modulated input and runs the blocks $\blocks$ only when the sum reaches~$\delta$; the residual
$\res_i$ of a computed step updates a damped-trend Kalman filter per DCT coefficient (the predictor), whose forecast
$\hat\res_i$ replaces it on skipped steps; $\Emb$ and $\Out$ run at every step.
\textbf{(b)}~When a computed step~$b$ ends the gap $a{+}1,\dots,b{-}1$, \pcgs spreads the innovation $e_b$ over the
gap with the RTS gains $g_i$, which grow toward~$b$; the smoothed residuals $\smooth{m}_i$ replace the extrapolation
$\prior{m}_i$.
\textbf{(c)}~Re-integrating the gap from $\lat_{a+1}$ with $\Emb$ and $\Out$ only lowers the latent deviation from full
computation at~$b$ by 25--59\% in all ten gaps of this sample (here $5.0\%\to2.4\%$ and $7.8\%\to3.2\%$), without
extra transformer evaluations.
(b, c): one \flux generation at $\delta=0.6$ for the DrawBench prompt whose PSNR gains (forecast over reuse,
and gap smoothing over the forecast) are closest to the benchmark means (``McDonalds Church.''); residual projected onto a fixed random unit direction~$\mathbf{u}$ in the lowest DCT
band (89\% of its energy); gray: full computation, evaluated only for this figure.}
\label{fig:method}
