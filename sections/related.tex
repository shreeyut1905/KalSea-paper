\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Training-free caching.}
Caching exploits the similarity of network activations at consecutive sampling steps. DeepCache~\cite{ma2024deepcache}
and block caching~\cite{wimbauer2024cache} reuse features of U-Nets; $\Delta$-DiT~\cite{chen2024deltadit},
FORA~\cite{selvaraju2024fora} and ToCa~\cite{zou2025toca} bring reuse to diffusion transformers at the level of blocks,
attention and MLP outputs, and tokens; PAB~\cite{zhao2025pab} and FasterCache~\cite{lv2025fastercache} target video
models, OmniCache~\cite{chu2025omnicache} distributes reuse over the sampling trajectory, and
Learning-to-Cache~\cite{ma2024learningtocache} learns which layers to cache. Recent caches decide online:
AdaCache~\cite{kahatapitiya2025adacache} adapts the schedule to each video, TeaCache~\cite{liu2025teacache}
accumulates the relative change of the timestep-modulated input of the first block and recomputes once it exceeds a
threshold, MagCache~\cite{ma2025magcache} models the magnitude ratio of successive residuals, and
DiCache~\cite{bu2026dicache} probes a shallow layer. \seacache~\cite{chung2026seacache} keeps TeaCache's
accumulate-and-reset rule but measures the change after a spectral-evolution-aware filter derived from the optimal
linear denoiser, so that the gate responds to content rather than noise. Fast ODE solvers~\cite{song2021ddim,lu2022dpmsolver,lu2025dpmsolverpp,zhao2023unipc,karras2022edm},
distillation~\cite{salimans2022progressive,song2023consistency,luo2023lcm,sauer2024add} and
quantization~\cite{li2023qdiffusion} are orthogonal to caching, which keeps the weights and the sampler unchanged.
We adopt the \sea gate unchanged and change
what is written on the steps it skips.

\paragraph{Forecasting and correcting cached features.}
TaylorSeer~\cite{liu2025taylorseer} replaces reuse by a Taylor extrapolation from finite differences of cached
features, HiCache~\cite{feng2026hicache} uses scaled Hermite polynomials, Spectrum~\cite{han2026spectrum} fits
Chebyshev polynomials by ridge regression, and FoCa~\cite{zheng2026foca} combines a BDF2 predictor with a Heun-type
calibration. Other methods check or repair what is written on skipped steps: SpeCa~\cite{liu2025speca} accepts or
rejects each forecast with a lightweight verification,
OmniCache~\cite{chu2025omnicache} filters the noise introduced by reused features, ERTACache~\cite{peng2026ertacache}
adjusts integration intervals and rectifies cache-induced errors with a closed-form model profiled offline, and
GP-Refiner~\cite{shen2026gprefiner} regresses the deviation of forecast features online with a Gaussian process. All
of them improve the value used at the \emph{current or upcoming} steps; none revises the latent of steps that have
already been skipped. \pcgs is complementary: it leaves the forecast for upcoming steps unchanged and corrects the past
gap. Contemporaneous with our work, SpecKCast~\cite{rajabi2026speckcast} tracks the DCT coefficients of the model
output with damped-trend Kalman filters along band-specific warped time and schedules computation by the filter's
uncertainty. Our predictor uses the same state-space model, but for the block-stack residual behind the \sea gate,
where the frequency decomposition is unnecessary (\cref{sec:ablations}). SpecKCast uses the filter only as a
predictor; \pcgs adds the smoother and the latent correction, which also improve reuse and linear extrapolation.

\paragraph{Filtering, smoothing and predictor-corrector integration.}
The Kalman filter~\cite{kalman1960new} and the RTS smoother~\cite{rauch1965maximum} are the exact recursive
estimators of linear-Gaussian state-space models~\cite{sarkka2013bayesian}. Our forecaster uses the integrated Wiener
process (constant-velocity) model of target tracking~\cite{barshalom2001estimation} with a damped
trend~\cite{gardner1985forecasting} and adapts its process noise from the innovations~\cite{mehra1972approaches}.
Probabilistic ODE solvers infer the solution of an ODE from evaluations of its vector field by Gauss--Markov filtering
and smoothing~\cite{schober2019probabilistic,tronarp2019probabilistic}; we instead use smoothing to infer the network
evaluations that a cache skipped. In diffusion sampling, predictor-corrector schemes add Langevin steps~\cite{song2021score} or a second evaluation
(Heun~\cite{karras2022edm}); UniPC~\cite{zhao2023unipc} corrects multistep solvers~\cite{lu2025dpmsolverpp} by reusing
the next evaluation, as \pcgs does, but \pcgs corrects a whole gap of skipped steps.
