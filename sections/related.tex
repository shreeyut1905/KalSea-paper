\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Accelerating diffusion sampling.}
Fast ODE solvers take fewer steps~\cite{song2021ddim,lu2022dpmsolver,lu2025dpmsolverpp,zhao2023unipc,karras2022edm},
distillation trains few-step students~\cite{salimans2022progressive,song2023consistency,luo2023lcm,sauer2024add},
and quantization~\cite{li2023qdiffusion} makes each evaluation cheaper. Caching leaves the sampler and the weights
unchanged and is orthogonal to these methods.

\paragraph{Training-free caching.}
Caches reuse activations across consecutive steps: U-Net features~\cite{ma2024deepcache,wimbauer2024cache}, blocks,
attention, MLP outputs and tokens of diffusion transformers~\cite{selvaraju2024fora,zou2025toca}, video-specific
features~\cite{zhao2025pab,lv2025fastercache}, or layers chosen over the trajectory~\cite{chu2025omnicache} or by
learning~\cite{ma2024learningtocache}. Adaptive caches decide online when to recompute, per
video~\cite{kahatapitiya2025adacache}, from the change of the first block's modulated input (TeaCache~\cite{liu2025teacache};
\seacache~\cite{chung2026seacache}, after a spectral-evolution-aware filter), from residual
magnitudes~\cite{ma2025magcache}, a shallow probe~\cite{bu2026dicache} or the stability of the
trajectory~\cite{jiang2025sada}. Forecasting caches extrapolate skipped outputs with Taylor~\cite{liu2025taylorseer},
Hermite~\cite{feng2026hicache} or Chebyshev~\cite{han2026spectrum} expansions or a BDF2 predictor~\cite{zheng2026foca};
others verify~\cite{liu2025speca} or rectify~\cite{peng2026ertacache,shen2026gprefiner} the written value, and
concurrent work~\cite{rajabi2026speckcast} also forecasts with Kalman filters. All of them improve the value used at
the \emph{current or upcoming} steps; none revises the latent of steps that have already been skipped.

\paragraph{Filtering, smoothing and predictor-corrector integration.}
The Kalman filter~\cite{kalman1960new} and the RTS smoother~\cite{rauch1965maximum} are the exact recursive estimators
of linear-Gaussian state-space models~\cite{sarkka2013bayesian}; probabilistic ODE solvers use them to infer the
solution of an ODE~\cite{schober2019probabilistic,tronarp2019probabilistic}. In diffusion sampling, predictor-corrector
schemes add Langevin steps~\cite{song2021score} or a second evaluation~\cite{karras2022edm}, and
UniPC~\cite{zhao2023unipc} corrects multistep solvers~\cite{lu2025dpmsolverpp} by reusing the next evaluation.
