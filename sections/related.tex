\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Accelerating diffusion sampling.}
Sampling can be accelerated by taking fewer steps, by distilling the model, or by making each evaluation cheaper.
Fast ODE solvers reduce the number of steps with higher-order or multistep
integrators~\cite{song2021ddim,lu2022dpmsolver,lu2025dpmsolverpp,zhao2023unipc,karras2022edm}. Distillation trains a
few-step student, \eg, by progressive distillation~\cite{salimans2022progressive}, consistency
models~\cite{song2023consistency,luo2023lcm} or adversarial distillation~\cite{sauer2024add}. Post-training
quantization~\cite{li2023qdiffusion} lowers the precision of weights and activations. These methods change the
sampler or the weights; caching leaves both unchanged and is orthogonal to them.

\paragraph{Training-free caching.}
Caching exploits the similarity of activations at consecutive sampling steps. Early caches reuse U-Net
features~\cite{ma2024deepcache,wimbauer2024cache} or the blocks, attention and MLP outputs, and tokens of diffusion
transformers~\cite{chen2024deltadit,selvaraju2024fora,zou2025toca}; others target video~\cite{zhao2025pab,lv2025fastercache},
distribute reuse over the trajectory~\cite{chu2025omnicache} or learn which layers to cache~\cite{ma2024learningtocache}.
Adaptive caches decide online when to recompute: AdaCache~\cite{kahatapitiya2025adacache} adapts the schedule to each
video, TeaCache~\cite{liu2025teacache} accumulates the relative change of the first block's timestep-modulated input
until it exceeds a threshold, MagCache~\cite{ma2025magcache} models the magnitude ratio of successive residuals, and
DiCache~\cite{bu2026dicache} probes a shallow layer. \seacache~\cite{chung2026seacache} measures this change after a
spectral-evolution-aware filter, so that the gate responds to content rather than noise.
Forecasting caches extrapolate the skipped outputs with Taylor expansions~\cite{liu2025taylorseer},
Hermite~\cite{feng2026hicache} or Chebyshev~\cite{han2026spectrum} polynomials, or a BDF2 predictor with Heun-type
calibration~\cite{zheng2026foca}, and other methods verify~\cite{liu2025speca} or
rectify~\cite{peng2026ertacache,shen2026gprefiner} the value written on skipped steps.
Concurrently, SpecKCast~\cite{rajabi2026speckcast} forecasts the DCT coefficients of the model output with
damped-trend Kalman filters and schedules computation by their uncertainty. All of them improve the value used at the \emph{current or upcoming} steps; none revises the latent of steps that have already been skipped.

\paragraph{Filtering, smoothing and predictor-corrector integration.}
The Kalman filter~\cite{kalman1960new} and the RTS smoother~\cite{rauch1965maximum} are the exact recursive
estimators of linear-Gaussian state-space models~\cite{sarkka2013bayesian}.
Probabilistic ODE solvers infer the solution of an ODE from evaluations of its vector field by Gauss--Markov filtering
and smoothing~\cite{schober2019probabilistic,tronarp2019probabilistic}. In diffusion sampling, predictor-corrector schemes add Langevin
steps~\cite{song2021score} or a second evaluation as in Heun's method~\cite{karras2022edm}; UniPC~\cite{zhao2023unipc}
corrects multistep solvers~\cite{lu2025dpmsolverpp} by reusing the next evaluation.
