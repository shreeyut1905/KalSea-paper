CVPR 2027 rules applied from the Author Guidelines (not in the kit)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
- 8 pages including figures and tables; extra pages only for references and the mandatory LLM Usage Statement.
- Mandatory LLM Usage Statement after the main text and before the references (sections/llm_statement.tex);
  it must not reveal the authors' identities; missing statements lead to desk rejection.
- Double-blind: no identifying information, no links to the authors' code (stating that code will be released is
  allowed). Supplementary material must be anonymous and may not link to external content.
