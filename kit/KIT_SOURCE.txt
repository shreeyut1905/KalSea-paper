Why this is not a CVPR 2027 kit (checked 2026-10-21)
----------------------------------------------------
- cvpr-org/author-kit has no 2027 tag or release. The newest tag is CVPR2026-v1(latex).
- main is 4 commits ahead of that tag (last commit 2026-05-28, "teaser w/ strip and preamble tweaks (#65)").
  Those commits touch only README.md, fig/teaser.tex, main.tex, preamble.tex and sec/2_formatting.tex.
  cvpr.sty and ieeenat_fullname.bst are identical on main and on the tag.
- https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines returns HTTP 404.
- The CVPR 2027 Call for Papers (posted 2026-09-03) does not link a template.
